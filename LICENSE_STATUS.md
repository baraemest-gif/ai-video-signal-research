# License & Reuse Status

## STATUS: UNRESOLVED FOR DOI RELEASE

This repository intentionally does **not** declare MIT, CC0, CC BY, ODC or another standard open-data/software license at this stage.

AI Video Signal already publishes custom Dataset License & Usage Terms for its research materials. The current research governance states that citation and limited editorial/research reuse are permitted with attribution and dated context, while full-file redistribution, mirroring and substantial competing commercial reuse remain subject to the published terms.

A standard machine-readable license must therefore **not** be inserted into `CITATION.cff`, `.zenodo.json`, Dataset structured data or a Zenodo deposit unless its rights actually match the public terms.

## Current safe state

- Repository access: public.
- Public access does not by itself grant an open-source or open-data license.
- DOI: not assigned.
- ORCID: not supplied.
- Standard machine-readable license: not selected.
- Custom public reuse terms: exist on AI Video Signal and must be preserved.
- Zenodo metadata: must omit a standard license until the mapping/decision is resolved.

## Decision required before first DOI

Choose one of these evidence-consistent paths:

1. **Keep custom terms** and deposit only if Zenodo metadata can accurately represent the access/reuse position without falsely claiming a standard open license; or
2. **Adopt an explicit standard data license** only after intentionally changing the public reuse policy to match that license; or
3. **Use a dual structure** in which factual dataset fields receive a defined standard license while protected editorial text, methodology expression, branding and/or other components remain under separate terms — only if the boundaries are explicit and legally/editorially intentional.

No option is selected by this document.

## Release gate

The first DOI release remains **HOLD** until:

- the applicable terms are unambiguous for every deposited file;
- the repository, website, citation metadata and Zenodo metadata do not contradict one another;
- any machine-readable license identifier matches the actual rights granted;
- redistribution and commercial-reuse language is internally consistent;
- the release package states what users may cite, copy, modify, redistribute and reuse commercially.

## Anti-overclaim rule

Do not describe the dataset as “open data,” “CC BY,” “CC0,” “open source,” or similarly licensed until the corresponding rights have actually been granted in the governing terms.
