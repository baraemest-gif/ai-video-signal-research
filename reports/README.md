# Reports

This directory is reserved for public research briefs, benchmark notes and release-specific analyses derived from citation-grade datasets.

## Publication standard

A report should identify:

- the exact research release used
- methodology version
- source and verification window
- which statements are provider-stated facts
- which numbers are AI Video Signal calculations
- which values are scenarios or assumptions
- material uncertainty and limitations

Reports must not imply hands-on testing when the evidence base is documentation review, and they must not convert ambiguous commercial-use language into definitive legal conclusions.

## Intended outputs

- AI Video Signal Global Benchmark releases
- AI video pricing and production-economics briefs
- market-change summaries
- commercial-rights evidence summaries
- methodology notes
- correction notes

A report may be updated editorially, but a report attached to an immutable research release should retain an immutable archived copy for citation.
