Establish a zero-point for measuring genuinely independent discovery, citation and reuse after the DOI-backed release was published.

## Counting rules

An item may count as an independent external citation/reuse only when all of the following hold:

1. It is published on a domain not controlled by AI Video Signal or the dataset creator.
2. It clearly refers to the AI Video Signal dataset, DOI, release, research findings or methodology.
3. It is independently published rather than a copied/syndicated duplicate of the same source.
4. The evidence is publicly retrievable or otherwise independently verifiable.
5. Provider fact-check replies do not count as independent media/research citations.
6. Sent outreach emails do not count as citations.
7. Search-result noise that merely shares the numeric DOI suffix does not count.

Target metrics remain separate from confirmed results.
